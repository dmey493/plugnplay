Analyze the Graph
MS-LS2-1 · In 1944, 29 reindeer were released on a remote Alaskan island with plenty of food and NO predators. The herd exploded to 6,000 — then almost vanished. What happened?
Part A · Analyze the Data
Describe the shape of the population line: when did the herd rise, peak, and crash?
What was the highest number of reindeer, and in what year? What was the lowest?
Look at the lichen column. What was happening to their food supply as the herd grew?
Part B · Claim · Evidence · Reasoning
Claim — answer this week's question in one sentence.

The reindeer herd crashed because…

Evidence — back it up with numbers from the graph and table.

For example, the herd grew to about ___ by 1963, but by 1966 only about ___ were left — after the lichen dropped from ___ to ___.

Reasoning — explain the science behind the data.

A population can grow fast while resources are plentiful, but food is limited, so once the reindeer ate the lichen faster than it could regrow, which is why…

Part C · Predict & Investigate
Predict: If a few reindeer had survived past 1966, what do you predict happens next — and why can’t the herd just grow back to 6,000 right away?
Investigate: Besides running out of food, what else could have hurt the herd (a hard winter, disease)? What data would help you decide the main cause?
