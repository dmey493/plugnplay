Part A · Analyze the Data
Which planet is the largest? Which is the smallest? About how many km across is each?
Compare the four rocky planets (Mercury–Mars) to Jupiter. About how many times wider is Jupiter than Earth?
The bars for the rocky planets are tiny next to Jupiter and Saturn. What does that tell you about scale in the solar system?
Part B · Claim · Evidence · Reasoning
Claim — answer this week's question in one sentence.

The gas giants are bigger than the rocky planets by about…

Evidence — back it up with numbers from the graph and table.

Reasoning — explain the science behind the data.

Part C · Predict & Investigate
Predict: Uranus and Neptune are called “ice giants.” Based on the pattern, would you expect a planet found even farther out to be large or small? Why is it hard to be sure?
Investigate: Diameter is only one way to compare planets. What other measurements (mass, distance from the Sun, number of moons) would help you group the planets?
